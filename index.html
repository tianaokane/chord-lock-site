What it does

Everything a songwriter
actually needs

🎤

Hum to find chords

Sing or hum your melody into the mic. Chord Lock detects pitch in real time and suggests chords that fit what you're hearing in your head.

🔒

Lock what works

Lock chords you love and the suggestions automatically narrow. No other tool does this — it's the feature that keeps your flow going.

📝

Lyrics + chord marks

Paste your lyrics and click to mark where chord changes happen. Clean, visual, and satisfying — like a real lead sheet.

🎸

Your tuning, your diagrams

Accurate fingering diagrams for standard, open G, DADGAD and more. See exactly where to put your fingers for your specific setup.

📄

Export as PDF

Turn your session into a clean chord chart you can print, share with your band, or file away in your songwriting archive.

✨

Duolingo-simple UI

No music degree required. Clean, friendly, step by step — building a song should feel like play, not fighting a DAW.

How it works

From melody in your head
to chart in your hand

1

Paste your lyrics

Drop in whatever you've written — a full verse, a rough sketch, even just a title. Click where you want chord changes to happen.

2

Hum or pick chords

Either hum the melody into your mic and let the app detect what notes you're singing, or manually pick chords from a visual grid.

3

Lock the ones you love

Like a chord? Lock it. The remaining suggestions automatically tighten around what's locked — so you're always building on solid ground.

4

Export your chart

When it feels right, export a clean PDF chord chart with accurate fingering diagrams for your tuning. Ready to play, record, or share.
